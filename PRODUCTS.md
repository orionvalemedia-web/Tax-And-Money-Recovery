# Tax & Money Recovery

All 4 products in this package.

Every entry below is taken from the package's product inventory. Nothing here is a plan or a
roadmap item; all of it is built.

---

## Devadex Escheat Recoverer

70 tests

Finds and recovers money that states are already holding for a business or an estate: unclaimed property, dormant accounts, uncashed cheques.

## Devadex Nexus Navigator

68 tests

Works out where a business owes sales tax, gets it registered in those states, and keeps up with the filings.

## Devadex R&D Credit Harvester

61 tests

Builds an audit-defensible federal research tax credit claim from payroll, project time and records the company already has.

## Devadex Certified Payroll (WH-347)

47 tests

Turns a week of job-site hours into a complete federal certified payroll form, plus data exports for state and city portals.
